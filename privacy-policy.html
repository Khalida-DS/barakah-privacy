Privacy Policy

Barakah — Smart Muslim Finance · Last updated: 30 September 2026

The short version: Barakah stores all of your financial information locally on your own device. We do not have accounts, we do not run servers that hold your data, and we never collect, transmit, sell, or share it. Your money stays on your phone.

Who we are

Barakah — Smart Muslim Finance ("Barakah", "the app", "we") is published by MindForge. If you have any question about this policy, contact us at ghsarah15@gmail.com.

What information the app handles

Everything you enter — income, expenses, debts, assets, accounts, savings goals, investments, and Zakat details — is stored only in your device's local app storage. This information never leaves your device and is not visible to us or to anyone else.

  • We do not require you to create an account or sign in.
  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or location.
  • We do not connect to your bank or any financial institution.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics, or tracking tools.
  • We do not send your data to any server, cloud service, or third party.

Data you export

The app lets you export your data (for example, as a backup or report file). When you do this, the file is created on your device and it is entirely under your control. If you choose to share, email, or upload that file, it leaves the app under your direction — we are not involved in and have no access to that transfer.

Permissions

Barakah requests only the permissions needed to function as an offline app. It does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, or precise location for data collection.

Third-party services

Barakah works fully offline. Components used to display charts and content are bundled inside the app; they do not send your data anywhere. The app is distributed through Google Play, which has its own privacy practices governing the download and update process; see Google's privacy policy for details.

Children's privacy

Barakah is a personal-finance tool intended for a general adult audience. It is not directed at children, and because the app does not collect any personal information, it does not knowingly collect information from children.

How to delete your data

Because your data lives only on your device, you are always in full control of it. You can remove all of it at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings, or by uninstalling the app. There is nothing stored elsewhere for us to delete.

Future features

If we later add optional features that involve syncing or transmitting data (for example, cloud backup), those features will be clearly described, will be something you choose to turn on, and this policy will be updated before they are released.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Religious guidance disclaimer

Barakah's Zakat and Islamic-finance tools are provided for educational and planning purposes only and are not a substitute for advice from a qualified scholar. Calculations show their method so you can verify them; please consult a trusted scholar for rulings specific to your situation.
